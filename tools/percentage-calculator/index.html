Percentage Calculator

A percentage calculator is a simple online tool used to calculate percentages quickly and accurately. It can help you find a percentage amount, calculate the original principal amount, or determine the percentage rate between two numbers.

What is a Percentage?

A percentage represents a number as a fraction of 100. The symbol used for percentage is %.

How to Calculate Percentage Amount

To calculate a percentage amount, multiply the principal amount by the percentage rate and divide the result by 100.

Percentage Amount = Principal Amount × Percentage Rate ÷ 100

How to Calculate Principal Amount

If you know the percentage amount and percentage rate, you can calculate the original principal amount.

Principal Amount = Percentage Amount × 100 ÷ Percentage Rate

How to Calculate Percentage Rate

To find what percentage one amount represents of another amount, multiply the amount by 100 and divide it by the principal amount.

Percentage Rate = Percentage Amount × 100 ÷ Principal Amount
