What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information stored by a website in your browser. They can help a website remember preferences and understand general usage.

How Cookies May Be Used

  • Supporting website functionality.
  • Understanding general traffic and usage.
  • Supporting advertising and measurement.

Third-Party Cookies

Third-party services used on some pages may set their own cookies. Their use of cookies is governed by their respective privacy policies.

Managing Cookies

Most modern browsers allow you to control or delete cookies through their settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.
